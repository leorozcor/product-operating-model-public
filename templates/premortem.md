# Pre-mortem: <team>, <term>

It is the end of the term and it went badly. Write down why, before the OKRs are frozen. 20 minutes.

1. **Everyone, silently, for 5 minutes:** the reasons it failed.
2. **Round the table:** one reason each until the list is complete.
3. **Pick the three most likely or most damaging.**
4. **For each:** what we would see early, and what we change now.
