# Sources and credits

This toolkit is inspired by a number of private and public sources: talks, practice inside several product organisations, internal guidance that is not published, books and courses. It restates ideas in its own words. Frameworks by others are credited here and paraphrased, not copied; private sources are not named or reproduced. Where an idea is Luis Orozco's own, it says so.

The licences (see `README.md`) cover this toolkit's own text and code. They grant no rights to the third-party frameworks, books and courses credited below.
