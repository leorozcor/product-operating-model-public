# Diagnose before installing

*Serves: 1.3 structure needs meaning; 1.4 soil before water; 2.3 autonomy needs five things at once; 3.8 start with the users' daily reality.*

Before introducing OKRs or changing teams, spend a few weeks learning how decisions actually get made. Write `diagnosis.md` with what you find, graded A, B, C or ?.

## Questions

**How the business works**
- What does the business actually make money from, and what product behaviour does that make rational?
- What is the company strategy, as people describe it in their own words? Do the descriptions match?

**How decisions get made**
- Who decides what a team works on? Who can stop it?
- Where do priorities really come from: strategy, sales escalations, the loudest customer, the last incident?
- Which decisions are reversible and treated as irreversible, or the other way round?

**The five conditions for autonomy** (belief 2.3), per team
- Autonomy: can the team choose how to solve the problem?
- Clarity: does it know which outcome matters and why?
- Sponsorship: does someone with authority protect it?
- Expertise: does it have, or can it reach, the skills it needs?
- Trust: can it bring bad news upward without punishment?

**Trust and information flow**
- When did bad news last travel upward quickly? What happened to the person who brought it?
- Are status reports mostly green? Are outcomes mostly missed anyway?

**Measurement**
- Can each team see what customers do with its product, or only what it ships? Who owns the data, and how long does a new metric take to appear?
- Mark teams that can't measure their outcomes as not instrumented (`maturity.md`).

**Capacity**
- Roughly how much capacity goes to strategic, tactical and operational work? Is there any slack?

**Teams**
- Are the teams self-contained and cross-functional, or do most changes need three teams?
- Are there fewer, real teams, or many nominal ones?

## Output

- The **limiting condition** for each team, and for the organisation.
- A **maturity level** for each team (see `maturity.md`), observed, with evidence.
- A recommendation on where to start, and on which beliefs in `BELIEFS.md` the leadership does not yet hold.
