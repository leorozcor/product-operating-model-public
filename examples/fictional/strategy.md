# Fernway company strategy (fictional)

*Owner: CEO. Last changed: 2026-12-10.*

## Purpose and vision
Every field-service visit ends with the job done.

## Where we play
Field-service firms with 20 to 300 technicians in the Nordics and Benelux. Not enterprise utilities, not single-person trades.

## How we win
| id | We believe that… | So we will… | By when | We'd be wrong if… | Evidence today |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Mid-size firms choose and keep software that raises first-visit completion | Make first-visit completion the product's promise and measure it for every customer | end 2027 | Retention doesn't differ between customers whose completion rose and those whose didn't | B: churn interviews |
| S2 | One billing platform lets us price per completed job, which customers prefer to per-seat | Move all customers to the new billing platform, then pilot per-job pricing | mid 2027 | Pilot customers choose per-seat when offered both | C: sales anecdotes |

## What we will not do
Build our own payments. Serve firms under 20 technicians this year.
