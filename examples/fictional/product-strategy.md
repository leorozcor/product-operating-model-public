# Fernway product strategy (fictional)

*Owner: CPO. Reviewed every term. Last changed: 2026-12-15.*

## Theme: Schedules dispatchers trust (serves S1)
**The problem.** Dispatchers rebuild the generated schedule by hand most mornings because it ignores technician skills and parts on the van (A: 14 shadowing sessions).
**Business value.** Dispatcher time is the cost customers feel; trust in the schedule drives renewal (B).
**User value.** The morning schedule is ready to send.
**Leading indicators.** Share of schedules sent without manual edits; dispatcher minutes per day in scheduling.
**First and next.** This term: skills and parts constraints. Next: live re-planning during the day.

| id | We believe that… | We'll know when… | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Adding skills and parts constraints makes dispatchers stop reworking schedules | Unedited schedules pass 55% | untested |

## Theme: Done on the first visit (serves S1)
**The problem.** 39% of jobs need a second visit; the top causes are missing information and missing parts (A: job data).
**Business value.** Second visits are unbilled cost for our customers; completion is our promise.
**User value.** Technicians arrive knowing what the job needs.
**Leading indicators.** First-visit completion; checklist completion before arrival.
**First and next.** This term: job checklists that work without signal. Next: parts prediction.

| id | We believe that… | We'll know when… | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| P2 | Technicians who see the full checklist before arrival complete more jobs first time | First-visit completion reaches 70% | untested |

## Theme: One billing platform (serves S2)
**The problem.** Two billing systems; per-job pricing is impossible on the old one.
**Business value.** Unlocks the per-job pricing pilot.
**User value.** One invoice, fewer billing errors.
**Leading indicators.** Customers migrated; billing tickets per 100 accounts.
**First and next.** This term: migrate everyone. Next: pricing pilot.
